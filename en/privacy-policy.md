# Privacy Policy — Numi Step

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

Numi Step is an Android educational app for practicing mathematics. The app follows an **offline-first** approach: local profiles, learning history, and results remain on the device, without requiring registration or a Numi Step account. This policy explains what information is stored, how Google Play is involved, and how users can manage their information.

## 1. Publisher and contact

The publisher of Numi Step is **Grzegorz Kalinowski**.

**Contact for privacy and personal data inquiries:** [numistep@kalinowski.me](mailto:numistep@kalinowski.me).

## 2. Information stored locally

Depending on the features used, the app may store on the device:

- names, selected avatars, and settings of local profiles;
- answers, session results, mistakes, statistics, achievements, certificates, and learning progress;
- app preferences, learning settings, and parental protection settings;
- information entered in generated documents, such as a name on a certificate or a PDF worksheet header;
- the last cached Premium entitlement state, used in part to provide offline access.

This information is stored locally using Android database and preference mechanisms (Room and DataStore). **Numi Step does not transmit educational data, profile names, or certificate contents to the publisher's server or analytics tools.** The publisher does not operate user accounts or a cloud database of learning progress.

## 3. Analytics, advertising, and crash reporting

The app contains no ads, analytics SDKs, or external crash reporting services such as Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Sentry. Numi Step does not use learning data for advertising profiling.

Android and Google Play services may independently process information relating to installations, device operation, or purchases, under their own policies. This does not mean that Numi Step sends them learning results.

## 4. Premium purchases through Google Play

Numi Step offers two optional Premium products:

- **Premium Monthly** — an automatically renewing monthly subscription;
- **Premium Lifetime** — a one-time purchase providing ongoing Premium access within the app.

Payments, renewals, and subscription cancellation are handled through **Google Play Billing**. The app communicates with Google Play to retrieve products and prices, identify purchased products, and check entitlement status. It may receive purchase-related information such as a product ID, purchase token, transaction status, and auto-renewal state. The last confirmed entitlement may be cached locally to support temporary offline use of Premium functionality.

Numi Step **does not receive your payment card number or Google Account password**. Google processes payment details. The publisher may see certain order and settlement information provided through Google Play Console for purchase support, inquiries, and legal obligations. The app does not attach learning data or local profiles to purchase communications.

Subscriptions can be managed in [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Details about Google's data processing are available in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Sharing documents at the user's request

The app can create, save, print, and share documents such as certificates and PDF worksheets. Sending a document to another app, service, or printer happens **only when the user chooses to do so**, such as using Android's sharing options. The recipient's policies apply to any further processing. The publisher does not automatically receive copies of these documents.

## 6. Contacting the publisher

If a user voluntarily emails [numistep@kalinowski.me](mailto:numistep@kalinowski.me), the publisher receives information included in that message, such as the sender's email address, message content, and any attachments. This information is used to respond, handle the inquiry, and, where necessary, comply with legal obligations or defend against claims. Please avoid sending children's personal information or detailed learning histories unless necessary.

## 7. Purposes and legal bases

Locally stored information enables the learning features requested by users. Purchase information is used to provide paid features and fulfill settlement obligations. Correspondence is used to respond to inquiries.

Where the GDPR applies, the applicable legal bases may include performance of a contract or a requested service (Article 6(1)(b)), compliance with a legal obligation including accounting obligations (Article 6(1)(c)), and legitimate interests in handling inquiries, maintaining security, and establishing or defending legal claims (Article 6(1)(f)). Google also processes payment information under its own legal bases.

## 8. Retention, backups, and deletion

Local profiles, progress, and settings remain on the device until the user deletes them (where the relevant function allows deletion) or clears the app's data in Android settings. Uninstalling the app ordinarily removes its local data. **Automatic Android cloud backup of Numi Step app data is disabled** in the app's configuration. Device-to-device transfer behavior may nevertheless depend on Android version and device manufacturer.

Once local data is deleted, it cannot be restored from a Numi Step server, because there is no such server. Uninstalling the app **does not cancel a Google Play subscription** or delete Google's purchase records.

Email correspondence is retained for the period necessary to respond and to meet any applicable legal obligations or legitimate claims-related needs. Purchase and settlement records available to the publisher are retained as required by applicable law and accounting obligations.

## 9. Security

Educational data remains on the device and is not automatically transmitted to the publisher's systems. Its security also depends on device and Android safeguards. We recommend using a screen lock, sharing documents containing personal information carefully, and supervising children's access to purchases in Google Play.

## 10. Children's privacy

Numi Step may be used by children under parental or guardian supervision. Children do not need to create accounts or provide email addresses to practice mathematics. Parents or guardians should decide what information is entered into local profiles, whether certificates are shared, and whether purchases are made. The app does not automatically transmit children's educational data to the publisher, advertisers, or analytics providers.

## 11. Data protection rights

Where the GDPR applies, users may, depending on the circumstances, have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict processing of, or obtain a portable copy of personal data, as well as to object to certain processing. Requests about data held by the publisher can be sent to [numistep@kalinowski.me](mailto:numistep@kalinowski.me).

Because the publisher cannot access local profiles and learning results, the publisher cannot remotely view, change, or recover those records. Users can manage them in the app or by clearing the app's data in Android settings. Google Account and payment data can also be managed using Google's tools.

Users may complain to the relevant data protection authority; in Poland, this is the **President of the Personal Data Protection Office** ([uodo.gov.pl](https://uodo.gov.pl/)).

## 12. Website hosting

If this policy is hosted using **GitHub Pages**, visiting the website involves connecting to GitHub infrastructure, which may process technical connection data under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The policy webpage itself contains no advertising or analytics scripts.

## 13. Changes to this policy

This policy may change if app features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at the same URL, and its update date will appear at the top.
